Aqua Mouse History

🏆 Aqua Mouse Hall of Fame

These mice lived in our store and are now sold. Each one is a piece of liquid mouse history — proof that the aqua aesthetic has been with us all along.

✕ Sold out · Gone forever
Fluvi Mouse
Zaragoza, Spain · Expo 2008
Fluvi was the official mascot of the Zaragoza 2008 World Expo, themed around water and sustainability. This mouse was a promotional item featuring a small Fluvi figurine floating in blue liquid. Only a few thousand were ever produced.
Expo 2008ZaragozaLimited edition

Care Bears Mouse
USA · circa 2004–2006
At the peak of the Y2K aesthetic era, Care Bears made a comeback with tech accessories. This red liquid mouse with a Care Bear figurine was sold in US toy stores around 2004–2006.

Россия Airlines Mouse
Russia · circa 2002–2005
A corporate promotional item from Rossiya Airlines. This blue liquid mouse houses a tiny airplane floating in clear liquid. Given to frequent flyers in the early 2000s.
Россия AirlinesRussiaRare

Kinder Surprise Mouse
Europe · circa 2001–2003
Ferrero produced this orange liquid mouse as a promotional item during a Kinder Surprise campaign in Europe. Never sold in stores — only gifted at promotional events. Ultra rare.
